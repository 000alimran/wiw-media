Nvidia just added nearly one trillion dollars in value. In one day.

Not because of earnings. Because one man came back.

On November twelfth, C.E.O. Jensen Huang resigned.

Reports said he had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort.

The stock was already down forty-one percent from its late-October high.

Last week, the board said it did not intend to bring him back.

Then, eleven days after he left, it reversed. Unanimously.

The stock jumped more than thirty percent. The biggest one-day value gain ever recorded.

Investors have a name for this: key-person risk.

When one person is worth a trillion dollars to a company,

that's a strength. And a risk.

The investigation isn't over. Follow for the next chapter.
